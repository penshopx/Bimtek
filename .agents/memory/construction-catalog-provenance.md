name: Construction catalog provenance
description: Source hierarchy and safe reconciliation of the official occupation catalog with legacy learning targets.
---

Use the uploaded SK DJBK's **new nomenclature** columns for reference occupations and schemes. Treat the Notion catalog as a product concept requiring reconciliation, not as an independently verified official master. Keep the ASN and fresh-graduate paths distinguishable from the general path.

**Why:** the decision's annex is a conversion table with old and new titles side by side, including merged and deleted occupations. Blind imports or name-based replacements can silently retain superseded roles or change a learner's saved target.

**How to apply:** preserve legacy target IDs and learning evidence until each mapping is explicitly reviewed. New reference entries must not imply their learning units, official requirements, or current LSP availability have already been verified. A source document is a dated snapshot, not proof of current scheme validity.
