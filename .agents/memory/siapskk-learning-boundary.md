name: SIAP SKK learning boundary
description: Keep SIAP SKK focused on learner preparation and separate from business administration.
---

SIAP SKK is a learner-facing platform for preparation, learning, and private reflection on work experience. Features inspired by Gustafta should be implemented as independent SIAP SKK features, not connected to Gustafta apps, accounts, APIs, or data. Keep contractor services, tender and administrative portals, and business operations outside the core learning flow. For SBU–SKK relationships, use verified sources and state uncertainty; do not imply an official mapping that has not been confirmed. AI guidance, project notes, and linked documents are not official assessment decisions or proof of competence.

**Why:** the user established SIAP SKK as a separate learning product and clarified that Gustafta-inspired features should be built separately inside SIAP SKK, without integration to Gustafta.

**How to apply:** when adapting ideas from the wider Gustafta ecosystem, implement only relevant learning/preparation capabilities natively in SIAP SKK. Use SIAP SKK's own accounts, storage, and APIs; do not add external Gustafta links or data flows as dependencies. Reuse modules within SIAP SKK where useful, and keep unrelated administrative services out of the learner workflow.
