---
name: SIGAP module boundaries
description: Product boundaries for the SIGAP UJK, PKB, and PUB blueprint and its relationship to SIAP SKK.
---

Treat SIGAP SKK as the organization-facing LMS/learning-management layer. Organizations manage members, assignments, learning paths, activities, and progress in SIGAP; workers consume learning content and practice through the learner-facing SIAP SKK app. Keep distinct UJK, PKB, and PUB modules within the broader SIGAP ecosystem. Shared participant identity, jabatan/competency context, learning evidence, and navigation can connect the products.

Keep organization operations and formal workflows behind separate permissions and clear ownership. The platform supports preparation and coaching; it must not present its readiness status as an official competence verdict, issue SKPK on its own, or claim an automatic government verification. Competence decisions belong to authorized assessors/LSP; PKB credit decisions belong to the authorized review process.

An imported account jabatan is preparation context, not evidence of an existing certificate. Keep intended/working jabatan separate from a user's self-reported current SKK; neither is a verified certification record.

**Why:** a jabatan saved while preparing documents expresses the user's chosen role, not proof that an LSP has issued the corresponding SKK.

**How to apply:** reuse account identity for preparation and coaching, but never automatically turn an intended role into a current or verified SKK.

**Why:** the product direction is for organizations to subscribe to SIGAP LMS while members learn in SIAP SKK; the user has prioritized desktop learning improvements and deferred mobile work. The blueprint also distinguishes learning/readiness support from regulated assessor, LSP, association, LPJK, and SIJK responsibilities.

**How to apply:** focus current SIAP work on the desktop learner journey; defer mobile. Keep the SIGAP organization portal and SIAP learner app distinct initially, then connect them through SSO and APIs for assignments, catalog, and progress; keep official status, credit estimates, review/audit trails, and organizational data separate and accurately labeled.

Do not turn unchecked blueprint credit formulas or module-completion points into official PKB targets or awarded SKPK. Use private estimates or learning progress until rules, supporting evidence, and review authority are established.

**Why:** the uploaded blueprint is a product concept, not proof that a learning module or manually entered activity already has recognized credit.

**How to apply:** preserve personal records, but separate estimates from verified credit and avoid pass/renewal claims from checklist completion or a points total.

When correcting expert-level labels, do not automatically promote generic job records (such as field supervisors or QC) to an expert level. Flag the job's exact scheme as needing confirmation if its authoritative mapping is unavailable.

**Why:** correcting an inconsistent label is not proof that the underlying job belongs to an expert scheme; automatic numeric replacement could silently reclassify the occupation.

**How to apply:** apply confirmed expert rank mappings to explicit expert records; obtain the authoritative job/qualification mapping before classifying generic job titles.

The user confirmed the conceptual SIAP SKK tutor model: PanduanASKOM is the main router/guide; specialist agents tutor within the learner's selected jabatan and jenjang. The intended journey is target selection → guide → specialist tutor → materials/practice → progress records. Do not treat this conceptual agreement as permission to create agents or change code, databases, or model configuration.

**Why:** the user explicitly confirmed the mapping and limited it to conceptual alignment, without implementation.

**How to apply:** use this model when discussing future learning assistance. Keep official-scheme claims conditional on verification; obtain an implementation instruction before building the mapped agents.
