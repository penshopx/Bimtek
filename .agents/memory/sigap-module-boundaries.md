---
name: SIGAP module boundaries
description: Product boundaries for the SIGAP UJK, PKB, and PUB blueprint and its relationship to SIAP SKK.
---

Treat SIGAP as one coherent platform with distinct UJK, PKB, and PUB modules. Shared participant identity, jabatan/competency context, learning evidence, and navigation can connect the modules, while organization operations and formal workflows need separate permissions and clear ownership.

The platform supports preparation and coaching; it must not present its readiness status as an official competence verdict, issue SKPK on its own, or claim an automatic government verification. Competence decisions belong to authorized assessors/LSP; PKB credit decisions belong to the authorized review process.

**Why:** the product blueprint explicitly distinguishes learning/readiness support from regulated assessor, LSP, association, LPJK, and SIJK responsibilities.

**How to apply:** when extending SIAP SKK, favor shared login and core profile with modular UJK/PKB/PUB areas; keep official status, credit estimates, review/audit trails, and organizational data separate and accurately labeled.
